The idea

The best things in your life came from a chance encounter.

A conversation. An introduction. A room you almost didn't walk into. We call it luck, but luck isn't random. It's a numbers game you can stack in your favor.

Shaping Luck is built on one idea:
increase the quality and frequency of your serendipitous encounters, and you change your trajectory. So we're guiding the encounters. You just bring the openness.
